Privacy Policy

How we handle information in connection with this website.

Last updated: September 2026

Overview

Cosminova Consulting (“Cosminova,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) operates this website to share information about our cosmetic quality and regulatory consulting services. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices available to you. It applies to this website only and not to any other communications with Cosminova.

Information We Collect

This website does not use cookies, tracking pixels, or third-party analytics, and it does not include any forms that collect personal information at this time.

If you contact us directly — for example, by emailing an address listed on our Contact page — we receive whatever information you choose to include, such as your name, email address, company, and the content of your message. We use this information only to respond to your inquiry and to provide the services you request.

As with most websites, our hosting provider may automatically log limited technical information from visitors, such as IP address, browser type, and pages viewed, for security and operational purposes. Cosminova does not have access to this data beyond standard, aggregated hosting analytics, and it is not used to identify individual visitors.

How We Use Information

  • To respond to inquiries and requests for consultation
  • To provide, administer, and improve our services
  • To comply with applicable legal obligations

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information to third parties.

Sharing of Information

We do not share information collected through this website with third parties, except where required to provide services you have requested, to comply with the law, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of Cosminova or others.

Data Retention

We retain correspondence and related information for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to your inquiry, maintain our business records, and comply with legal obligations.

Your Rights

Depending on where you are located, you may have rights regarding the personal information you have shared with us, including the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of that information. California residents have rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), and residents of the European Union and United Kingdom have rights under the GDPR and UK GDPR. To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below.

Children's Privacy

This website is intended for business audiences and is not directed to individuals under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time, including as this website adds new functionality such as contact forms or analytics. The “Last updated” date above reflects the most recent revision.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle information, please contact us at info@cosminova-consulting.com.
