Background

MoCRA Requirements & Effective Dates

The Modernization of Cosmetics Regulation Act (MoCRA) was signed into law on December 29, 2022 — the first major reform of U.S. cosmetic law since 1938. Its requirements are phasing in on a defined timeline.

Dec 2023

Safety & Adverse Events

Manufacturers must substantiate product safety and maintain adequate records. Responsible persons must keep adverse event records for six years (three for small businesses) and report serious adverse events to the FDA within 15 business days.

Jul 2024

Facility & Product Registration

Facilities manufacturing or processing cosmetics for U.S. distribution must register with the FDA and renew every two years. Responsible persons must list each marketed product, including ingredients and facility details.

Dec 2024

Labeling & Transparency

Labels must include contact information for reporting adverse events and disclose fragrance allergens. Professional-use products must state their restriction to licensed professionals.

Ongoing

Good Manufacturing Practice (GMP)

The FDA is establishing mandatory GMP regulations for cosmetic facilities, expected to align closely with ISO 22716 principles.

Effective dates reflect the general MoCRA framework and are provided for informational purposes only. Contact us to confirm how they apply to your facility or products.
