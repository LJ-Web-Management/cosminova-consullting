About Cosminova

Quality and Regulatory Guidance, Grounded in Practice

A boutique consultancy built to bring quality, regulatory, and market-entry considerations together within a single engagement.

Our Firm

Based in Los Angeles, Working Across the U.S., EU, and UK

Cosminova Consulting is a cosmetics quality and regulatory consultancy supporting cosmetic brands and contract manufacturers with GMP auditing, regulatory compliance, and quality system development.

Our practice is grounded in hands-on auditing against ISO 22716 and practical command of the Modernization of Cosmetics Regulation Act (MoCRA), FDA requirements under 21 CFR, California requirements, Regulation (EC) No 1223/2009, and UK cosmetics legislation.

We are building toward a single point of contact for brands entering new markets — bringing quality, regulatory, and coordination with legal counsel together within one engagement, so our clients can move forward with clarity instead of navigating each requirement separately.

Our Approach

  • Hands-on, audit-based experience rather than generic checklists.
  • Clear, plain-English explanations of regulatory requirements.
  • Practical recommendations sized to each client’s stage and resources.
  • Coordination with independent legal counsel where legal matters arise.
